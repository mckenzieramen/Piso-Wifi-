IMPORTANT:
The browser-only Firebase Auth reset flow can trigger Firebase's password-reset email, but it cannot replace Firebase's server-side email renderer with arbitrary custom HTML. To make the actual delivered email match the HTML preview exactly, configure the Firebase Authentication email template or use a server-side email provider.
