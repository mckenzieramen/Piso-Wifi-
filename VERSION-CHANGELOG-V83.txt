V83 — Schedule Data Integrity + Saved Week View + Unassigned Employee Fix

- New employees are created with NO site and NO pre-assigned weekly schedule.
- Employee Today's Site now follows saved weeklySchedules only; no fallback to a default site.
- Blank/unscheduled days are treated as unscheduled/off for attendance/payroll eligibility until a schedule is explicitly saved.
- Schedule Week / Date is normalized to Monday and always renders the full 7-day Monday–Sunday period.
- Added a Saved Weeks area so previously saved weekly schedules can be selected and viewed again.
- New weeks remain blank until explicitly saved; previous weeks are preserved.
- Existing attendance, Time In/Out, sales, payroll, employee, and face data are not deleted or reset by these changes.
